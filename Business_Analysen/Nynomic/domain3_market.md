## Gaps

- **External market size figures not cited in Nynomic reports**: The FY2025 and H1 2026 reports do not cite any external market research firm's TAM or CAGR estimates for photonics, spectroscopy, or sensor markets. All market-size figures in this table come from third-party market research (Future Market Insights, Coherent Market Insights, Precedence Research) and not from Nynomic's own published statements.
- **Direct competitor financial comparisons**: Only Jenoptik (much larger, EUR 1.0B+ revenue) is publicly traded and easily comparable. Mid-cap optical measurement competitors (Micro-Epsilon Optronic, Polytec, Gigahertz Optik, LaVision, Polytec) are either private, not in the same segment, or lack public current-year financials for a clean FY2025 side-by-side comparison.
- **P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples for Nynomic and peers**: Current trading multiples are not cited; only 12-month price targets and historical analyst targets are available. A calculated current P/E would require current market cap and net income, which are not verified here.
- **Analyst coverage breadth**: Only 2 analysts cover Nynomic. This is below the 3-analyst threshold often used to establish "consensus"; thin coverage reduces the reliability of a collective price target.

## Contradictions

- **Analyst price targets: March 2026 vs. June 2026 updates**: The FY2025 Annual Report (dated March 2026 retrieval) cites Montega EUR 18.00 and NuWays EUR 21.00 targets. Web searches (June 2026 date on MarketScreener) show updated targets of Montega EUR 27.00 and NuWays EUR 25.00 (and NuWays also cited as EUR 25.00 in another source). The discrepancy suggests analysts revised targets upward during H1 2026, likely following positive H1 results and management guidance confirmation.

## Raw notes

**Nynomic's own strategic emphasis:**
- Management explicitly frames the company as benefiting from "long-term growth trends in photonics" (FY2025 AR p. 325).
- The combination of optical measurement + AI is positioned as a key growth lever ("becoming a key driver of innovation and opening up additional growth opportunities across our value chain").
- NyFIT2025 is presented as a _temporary_ drag on earnings (one-time EUR 1.5M cost) with a payoff of EUR 5-6M annual savings by 2026, supporting management's target of 6-8% EBIT margin in FY2026.
- Order backlog increase of 46% (to EUR 63.5M as at 30 June 2026) is highlighted by management as evidence of market recovery and demand momentum; this is a leading indicator for revenue in H2 2026 and 2027.

**Analyst positioning:**
- Thin coverage (2 analysts) is typical for a scale-segment stock (Deutsche Börse Scale 30 index, small-cap liquidity). The "Strong Buy" consensus reflects both analysts' optimism, but the narrow base means consensus can shift quickly on a single revision.
- Target prices cited (EUR 25-29 as of June 2026) imply roughly 100-150% upside from the FY2025 close price (EUR 9.84 on 30.12.2025, per AR p. 1027), but the stock has recovered to ~EUR 26 by mid-2026 (current analyst range), narrowing that gap.

**Peer profile:**
- Jenoptik is 11× Nynomic's size by revenue (EUR 1.0B vs. EUR 92M, FY2025) but similar in profitability challenge: Jenoptik's EBIT margin fell from 13.1% (2024) to 10.9% (2025), a 2.2pp drop. Both companies are in cyclical, capex-driven end markets (semiconductors, photovoltaics, pharma R&D) and faced 2024-2025 headwinds.
- Jenoptik's 2026 guidance (single-digit revenue growth, EBIT margin ~3% despite cost cuts) shows less optimism than Nynomic's (6-8% EBIT margin target), possibly reflecting Jenoptik's larger defense/medical diversification buffering but also heavier legacy burden.

**Market narrative (third-party research):**
- Photonics market CAGR figures vary (4.16%-6.69%) based on scope definition (components-only vs. systems+services; optical sensors vs. total photonics). Optical sensor CAGR (8.3%) exceeds total photonics, consistent with sensor miniaturisation and IoT adoption trends cited in management commentary.
- The fact that Nynomic does not cite these market research firms in its reports (unlike some peers who reference IDC, Gartner, etc.) suggests either (a) market research does not drive investor-facing narratives at Nynomic, or (b) the company views its internal market intelligence (order pipeline, customer feedback) as more actionable than third-party forecasts.
