**Definition:** Umicore states ROCE but does not provide denominator in the documents scanned. Typically ROCE = Adjusted EBIT / (Avg Capital Employed = Equity + Net Debt). Verification against stated figures:
- FY2025: 15.7% × (€2.272bn equity + €1.4bn net debt) ≈ €582m implied EBIT ≈ €579m stated adjusted EBIT. **Matches.**

| FY2022 | 16.0 | 570 | 3,566 (year-end) | Calc: 570/3,566 = 16.0% |

**Note:** FY2025 ROE calculated using adjusted net profit (€288m) over average equity (€2.27bn [2024 end] + €3.697bn [2023 end])/2 = €2.984bn; ROE = 288/2.984 = 9.7%. **Using year-end equity (€2.27bn): 288/2.27 = 12.7%.**

Different denominators yield:
- Year-end equity: 12.7%
- Average equity (prior-year + current-year): 9.7%
- Implied from ROCE (cap employed): ~10.6% (if ROCE of 15.7% applies to broader capital base)

**ROCE vs ROE divergence (FY2025):** ROCE 15.7% > ROE 10-13% suggests Umicore is generating returns above equity cost but may have leverage component or capital efficiency gains from non-equity sources. Likely reflects that capital employed includes debt, and net debt carrying cost is lower than the ROCE hurdle after tax.

## Notes for IRR Modeling

### Critical Input Flags for Cash Flow / IRR Model

1. **EBITDA trajectory volatile:** €1.15bn (FY2022) → €972m (FY2023) → €763m (FY2024) → €847m (FY2025). Rebound FY2025 from cost actions + metal prices, not volume recovery. FY2026 guidance qualitative only (no targets).

2. **Battery Materials drag:** Segment remains unprofitable and capital-intensive. Management targets value recovery (defined as "positive adjusted EBITDA by 2026"). Medium-term guidance states "€1.0-€1.2bn EBITDA at €275-€325m EBIT by 2028" for Battery Cathode Materials (AR2025, p.33), but this requires assumptions on customer ramp, take-or-pay contracts, and capex payoff (currently €310m annual capex in constrained environment, expected to increase).

3. **Leverage & debt service:** Net debt €1.4bn (1.60x EBITDA FY2025), down from €1.87x FY2024. Debt structure includes €499m USPP (7–12 yr maturity) drawn July 2024. Interest expense rose to €114m FY2024 from €101m FY2022. Modeling cash taxes: effective tax rate 29% adjusted (FY2024); IFRS rate distorted by impairment timing.

4. **Free cash flow definition:** Umicore's "Free Operating Cash Flow" (€524m FY2025) does NOT equal OCF – capex (which = €849m – €325m = €524m). It appears to account for working capital normalization and exclude certain one-offs. Use OCF-reported-capex for conservative base case.

5. **Segment capital intensity:** Recycling and Catalysis are lower-capex, high-FCF generators. Battery Materials is high-capex (€80–€150m annually expected through 2028) with delayed payoff. Model these separately for break-even/IRR analysis.

6. **Dividend sustainability:** Dividend halved to €0.50 (€60m annually on ~240m shares). At FY2025 FCF of €524m, payout ratio = 11% — sustainable unless FCF deteriorates >50%. Prior €0.80 DPS (€192m) was maintained despite FY2024 loss; reinstatement contingent on Battery Materials improvement + Recycling stability.
