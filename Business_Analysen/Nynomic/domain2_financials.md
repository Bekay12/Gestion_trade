### Guidance vs. Actual

**FY2025:**
- **Guidance issued at year start (included in FY2024 report or Q4 2024 corporate communications):** Revenue EUR 105.0–110.0 m; EBIT EUR 8.5–10.0 m.
- **Revised guidance issued November 2025 (during the year):** Revenue EUR 93.0–96.0 m; EBIT EUR ~2.0 m.
- **Actual FY2025:** Revenue EUR 92.6 m; EBIT EUR 1.96 m. *Actual fell slightly below revised range but matched adjusted forecast.*
- **Reason for revision:** Market environment challenged by geopolitical uncertainties, weak industrial demand, delayed investment decisions, US tariffs, customer inventory reduction. Specific weakness in Clean Tech segment (-13% revenue).
- **NyFIT2025 impact:** Restructuring expenses (quantified separately in report but embedded in EBIT figure) contributed to margin compression. Program expected to generate positive effects from 2026 onwards.

**FY2024:**
- **Guidance (from FY2023 report):** Not explicitly extracted but FY2023 report discusses "further expansion of EBIT margin" and "stable double-digit EBIT margins" as strategic goals.
- **Actual FY2024:** Revenue EUR 102.4 m; EBIT EUR 7.4 m (7.2% margin). Partial recovery from FY2023 peak but pressured by discontinued operations charge (-4.0 EUR m) and market normalization post-COVID.

**FY2023:**
- **Guidance:** Peak performance year; EBIT EUR 15.4 m, margin 13.1%, net income EUR 10.5 m. Described as "strong earnings performance" with "stable double-digit EBIT margins."
- **Actual:** Achieved as reported.

**FY2026 (Guidance):**
- **Revenue:** EUR 100.0–105.0 m (midpoint 102.5 m, ~11% recovery from FY2025's 92.6 m).
- **EBIT margin:** 6–8% (midpoint 7%, implying EUR 7.2–8.4 m EBIT at midpoint revenue).
- **Assumptions:** NyFIT2025 efficiencies realized; order backlog strong; H1 2026 showing momentum (revenue +13% vs. H1 2025, EBIT positive).
