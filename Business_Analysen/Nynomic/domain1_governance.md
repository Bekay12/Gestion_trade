## Raw notes

**Board continuity**: The Management Board and Supervisory Board have remained completely unchanged from FY2022 through FY2025 (three-year period verified). No departures, appointments, or interim changes reported.

**Supervisory Board activity**: The report explicitly states that the Supervisory Board met on two occasions during FY2025: 6 May 2025 and 9 December 2025 (page 16). Both meetings were devoted to reviewing annual financial statements, management reports, and audit findings. No special committees were established during the financial year (page 16).

**Remuneration asymmetry**: Supervisory Board remuneration is disclosed in full (EUR 83k annual total for 3 members), but Management Board remuneration is withheld under the §314(3)/§286(4) HGB exemption for small/medium-sized corporations. This is a standard German disclosure practice, not an indicator of non-compliance.

**Directors' Dealings context**: The April 2025 share acquisitions by Board members (EUR 100k-999k range estimated from "six-figure euro range" language) occurred during market weakness (Nynomic share declined from EUR 19.30 on 02.01.2025 to EUR 9.84 on 30.12.2025, page 26). The timing suggests confidence in recovery, supported by later analyst target price revisions to EUR 18.00 (Montega) and EUR 21.00 (NuWays) as of March 2026 (page 26).

**Auditor tenure**: Clauß Paal & Partner is the third-year auditor for Nynomic (referenced in FY2023-2025 reports). Lead auditors Felix Hovelbernd and Stefan Evers are identified as CPAs (Wirtschaftsprüfer) and Tax Advisers (Steuerberater). Audit report dated 23 April 2026, issued without reservations (page 77).

**Group structure clarification**: The report states "With ten subsidiaries worldwide" (page 20), but the consolidation table on page 36 lists 24 consolidated subsidiaries total (10 domestic + 14 foreign), plus 1 associate. The "ten subsidiaries worldwide" phrase refers to operating subsidiaries with direct revenue-generating activities (Sill Optics, secopta, NIRON, TactiScan, m-u-t, APOS, Spectral Engines, Sensortherm, Photecture, LemnaTec, etc. per business segment descriptions, pages 19-21), while the full consolidation includes holding companies and intermediate entities.

**No governance disputes reported**: The Supervisory Board's section "Changes in the composition of the Supervisory Board and the Management Board" explicitly states "There were no changes in the composition of either the Supervisory Board or the Management Board during the 2025 financial year" (page 17).
