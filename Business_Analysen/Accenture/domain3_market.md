## Gaps

- **Operating margin for Capgemini and Cognizant:** Not obtainable from freely available sources (Yahoo Finance displays net profit margin only). Substituted net profit margin for comparability, but these differ across firms with different tax rates and financial structure. Prevents direct margin comparison with Accenture's 14.7% GAAP operating margin.
- **IT services / consulting market size (2024–2025):** Gartner, IDC, and Statista data are behind subscription paywalls. No public estimate obtained. Accenture's ~$70B revenue cannot be contextualized against market size or growth rate without this figure.
- **Analyst consensus rating split:** Only mean price target and one individual rating (Goldman Sachs) obtained. Full buy/hold/sell distribution not retrieved. Yahoo Finance UI displays this but did not extract into fetch result.
- **Peer operating margins:** Both Capgemini (CAP.PA) and Cognizant (CTSH) operating margin not disclosed in public financial data accessible via Yahoo Finance; net profit margin used instead, which conflates tax effects and non-operating items.
- **Segment revenue breakdown beyond geography:** 10-K Item 7 provides 5 industry groups (Communications Media & Technology, Financial Services, Health & Public Service, Products, Resources) but no operating income by industry or margin by service line (consulting vs. managed services margin). This limits ability to assess competitive position by strategic segment.
- **Client concentration and churn:** 10-K Item 1A states "a small number of clients have contributed…a significant portion" but no quantitative threshold (top 10 customer %) or actual churn rate disclosed.
- **Competitive win/loss rates:** No disclosed win rates vs. specific competitors; cannot assess pricing power or market share gains/losses at deal level.

## Contradictions

- **Revenue figure discrepancy (10-K vs. TTM):** 10-K Item 7 shows FY2025 total revenues of $69,673 million (ended 2025-08-31). Yahoo Finance TTM (~2025-10) shows $74.18 billion. Discrepancy (~$4.5B) attributable to partial FY2026 results included in TTM (Oct 2025 Yahoo date vs. Aug 31, 2025 10-K cutoff). **Resolution:** 10-K figure is official full fiscal-year GAAP; TTM is forward-looking and includes partial Q1 FY2026. Both are correct in their respective contexts.

## Raw notes

- **Organizational change (Sept 1, 2025):** Accenture moved from "five operating groups" (Communications, Technology, Financial Services, Health & Public Service, Products, Resources) to a single integrated "Reinvention Services" unit reporting by geographic market only. This is a major structural change reported in Item 1, Business—not yet reflected in full year segment reporting in FY2025 10-K (which closes Aug 31, before the change). Future 10-Ks will show the new structure.

- **Segment reporting:** The 10-K treats geographic markets (Americas, EMEA, Asia Pacific) as the three "reportable operating segments" per FASB guidance. Industry group revenue is disclosed (5 groups shown in MD&A) but there is no operating income (margin) by industry group—only by geography. This limits competitive analysis by industry or service line.

- **Managed services vs. consulting mix:** 10-K Item 1 notes that managed services contracts have longer multi-year terms and typically require longer termination notice. This suggests recurring revenue predictability is higher for managed services. However, no % breakdown of consulting vs. managed services revenue, nor separate margins for each, is provided in the filing. Yahoo Finance-available metrics are company-wide and do not separate by service type.

- **Competitive statements verified to claim only:** Item 1, Business, Competition section states: "We believe no other company offers the full range of services at scale that Accenture does." This is management's belief, not backed by a named competitor comparison or third-party validation. It is a position statement, not a verifiable competitive advantage.

- **AI investment narrative:** 10-K cites "early and decisive decision in fiscal 2023 to invest significantly to become a leader in generative AI with a $3 billion multi-year investment." This is stated as forward-looking strategy, not a completed measurement of outcome. No market share, revenue, or client adoption metrics for AI solutions are provided.

- **Workforce development as competitive asset:** The 10-K emphasizes 47 million training hours (9% YoY increase), 97,000 promotions, and 75% "great place to work" satisfaction as differentiators. These are effort/outcome metrics, not market-leading proof (peer training investment or satisfaction not compared).

- **Federal Services concentration:** 8% of total revenue (FY2025), 15% of Americas revenue, 36% of Health & Public Service revenue. This concentration risk is disclosed but not flagged as a vulnerability in Item 1 Business section; it appears only in Item 1A Risk Factors discussion of government contracting environment.

- **TTM vs. fiscal-year mismatch:** Yahoo Finance data is ~Oct 2025 (TTM), while 10-K is Aug 31, 2025 (full fiscal year). They are not directly comparable; TTM includes partial Q1 FY2026. When citing figures, always specify source and date to avoid confusion.
