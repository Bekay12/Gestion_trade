## Gaps

Recovery likely varies by metal and feed stream. No consolidated recovery rate figure found in primary sources.

Market is competitive but not explicitly mapped in Umicore's report.
- **CORE 2028 intermediate milestones**: Plan states ambitions for 2028 but does not publish quarterly/annual targets for 2026-2027 (only FY2025 results and FY2025-2028 cumulative cash flow targets given).
- **Battery Cathode Materials break-even mechanics**: Report states "break-even adjusted EBITDA" achieved in 2025 vs. "close to break-even in 2024 with €40M positive one-offs," but does not isolate underlying unit economics (volume ramp, margin per kg, customer concentration).

## Raw notes

### Business model (pages 5-12, AR2025)
Umicore describes itself as "advanced materials and recycling company" with four business groups:
1. **Battery Materials Solutions** (€436M rev, -€21M EBITDA 2025): Comprises Battery Cathode Materials and Battery Recycling Solutions. Focus on "value recovery" amid EV industry challenges.
2. **Catalysis** (€1,668M rev, €450M EBITDA 2025): Three units—Automotive Catalysts, Fuel Cell & Stationary Catalysts, Precious Metals Chemistry. Described as "reducing harmful emissions."
3. **Recycling** (€947M rev, €371M EBITDA 2025): Three units—Precious Metals Refining, Jewelry & Industrial Metals, Precious Metals Management. Recovers 17 critical metals; 25-year track record. Unique Hoboken (Belgium) facility.
4. **Specialty Materials** (€558M rev, €108M EBITDA 2025): Germanium, Metal Deposition Solutions, Cobalt & Specialty Materials.

**Key insight**: Catalysis is the EBITDA leader (€450M), not Recycling (€371M)—contrary to secondary press coverage suggesting Recycling is "dominant." Recycling is financially strong but second to Catalysis.

### Strategy: CORE 2028 (pages 10-12, AR2025)
- Launched March 2025
- Four strategic imperatives: Capital, Performance, People & Culture, Partnerships
- Focus on "maximizing cash generation" in foundation businesses (Catalysis, Recycling, Specialty Materials)
- "Recovering value" in Battery Cathode Materials
- Efficiency target for 2025: €100M—achieved per press release
- 2028 group targets: adjusted EBITDA €1.0-1.2B with margin > 23%; ROCE > 15%; capital spend limit €2.1B for 2025-2028

### Battery Materials weakness (page 17-18, AR2025; Resource Recycling)
- Root cause per Umicore: "Challenges in the EV industry" + "slowness of EV adoption rate"
- Summer 2024 announcement: "sharp slowdown in growth of demand for EVs" led to "steeply declined" customer demand projections
- Response: Halt/reduce capacity expansion. CEO stated "halving" capital between 2025-2028 for battery projects; invest only to finish existing projects and honor customer take-or-pay contracts
- Battery Cathode Materials achieved break-even adjusted EBITDA in 2025 (vs. -€40M underlying in 2024, before one-offs)
- Battery Recycling Solutions losses reduced substantially through "diligent cost management"

### Regulatory tailwind: EU Battery Regulation
- Not explicitly named in AR2025 but mentioned in sustainability section as "battery regulations"
- From external sources: Mandatory recycled content specs take effect 2030 (12% Co, 4% Li, 4% Ni, 85% Pb). 2036 higher (20% Co, 10% Li, 12% Ni). Reporting phase 2026-2029.
- Logical tailwind for Umicore Recycling (recovers 17 metals, can supply recycled Co/Ni/Li) but Umicore does not quantify potential revenue uplift

### Peer comparison
**Johnson Matthey PGM Services** (FY 2024/25 = year ended 31 March 2025):
- Revenue: £464M
- Operating profit: £149M
- EBITDA margin: 38.1%
- Position: World's largest PGM recycler; direct competitor to Umicore Recycling (Precious Metals Refining sub-unit)
- Umicore Recycling (2025): €947M rev, €371M EBITDA = 39.2% margin. Larger absolute and similar margin.

**Glencore** (FY 2025, full conglomerate):
- Total revenue: $247.5B (not battery metals specific; limited public data on battery/recycling sub-segment)
- Adjusted EBITDA: $13.5B
- Operates EVR (battery metals recycling JV with Redwood) but segment not separately disclosed
- Not a clean peer for Umicore on margins/strategy; Glencore is diversified mining/trading; Umicore is specialized materials tech

### Analyst coverage
- Official count: 14 analysts per Umicore investor relations page
- Consensus (per Investing.com/MarketScreener): "Buy" (9 buy, 1 sell, 5 hold out of ~15 analysts)
- 12-month price target: €23.79 average (range €15-€33)
- Implication: Moderate consensus optimism; analyst base is substantial (14-15 vs. thin-cap small-cap coverage), so consensus deserves weight

### Circular economy messaging
- Umicore emphasizes "circular business model" throughout: metals → products → end-of-life → recycled metals → new products
- 2025 achievement: > 50% of metal-containing input came from non-primary (recycled/secondary) sources across Group
- Nexyclus™ offering: branding for fully recycled PGMs with traceability guarantee
- Strategic positioning: Resource scarcity + regulatory pressure (EU Battery Reg, extended producer responsibility) support long-term Recycling growth
